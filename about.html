Our Story

From a Small Workshop to Pakistan's Trusted Builder

Founded in 2008 by Eng. Syed Jamil Bukhari, SJB C&C started as a small contracting firm with a big dream — to redefine construction standards in Pakistan. What began with a team of 5 dedicated professionals has grown into one of the region's most respected construction companies.

Over 18 years, we have successfully delivered over 620 projects spanning residential, commercial, industrial, and infrastructure sectors. Our journey has been defined by an unwavering commitment to quality, safety, and client satisfaction that has earned us the trust of over 250 clients.
