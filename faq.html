General Questions

What services does SJB C&C offer?

SJB C&C offers a comprehensive range of construction services including residential construction, commercial construction, industrial construction, interior design, renovation & remodeling, architecture & design, civil contracting, and full project management. We handle projects from initial concept through to final handover.

How long has SJB C&C been in business?

SJB C&C was founded in 2008 and has been serving clients across Pakistan for over 18 years. In that time, we have successfully completed 620+ projects and built a reputation as one of the region's most trusted construction companies.

Which cities does SJB C&C operate in?

We currently have active operations in Lahore, Islamabad, Faisalabad, and Multan. Our headquarters is in Lahore, Punjab. We also take on special projects in other cities across Pakistan on a case-by-case basis.

Cost & Payment

How much does it cost to build a house in Pakistan?

Construction costs vary significantly based on location, design complexity, materials chosen, and finishes required. As a general guide, basic construction starts from PKR 4,500 per sq ft, mid-range from PKR 6,500 per sq ft, and premium/luxury from PKR 9,000+ per sq ft. We provide detailed, transparent quotations after an initial consultation and site assessment.

What payment options do you accept?

We accept bank transfers, cheques, and online payments. Our standard payment structure is milestone-based: typically 15-20% advance, followed by progress-linked payments at key construction milestones. We provide detailed invoices at every stage so you always know exactly what you are paying for.

Is the initial consultation free?

Yes, our initial consultation is completely free and comes with no obligations. We will visit your site, discuss your vision, understand your requirements, and provide a preliminary estimate. This helps both of us determine if we are a good fit for your project.

Timeline & Process

How long does a typical construction project take?

Timeline depends on project size and complexity. A standard residential house (5 Marla to 1 Kanal) typically takes 10-14 months. Larger homes and commercial buildings can take 18-30 months. We provide a detailed project schedule with milestones during the planning phase and keep you updated throughout the construction process.

Do you handle all necessary approvals and permits?

Yes, we handle all necessary building approvals, permits, and regulatory compliance. This includes local authority permissions, structural approvals, environmental clearances, and any other documentation required by law. Our team has extensive experience working with municipal authorities across Punjab.

Warranty & Materials

What warranty do you provide on your construction work?

We provide a comprehensive structural warranty of 10 years on all our construction projects, covering the foundation, structure, and load-bearing elements. Additionally, we offer a 2-year warranty on finishes, fittings, and non-structural elements. Any issues arising within the warranty period are addressed promptly at no additional cost.

What quality of materials does SJB C&C use?

We exclusively use premium-grade materials from trusted manufacturers. Our standard specifications include Bestway/Lucky cement, Grade 60 steel, A-grade bricks, and ISI-certified electrical and plumbing fixtures. We also offer upgraded material packages for clients who want specific brands or premium finishes.

Is SJB C&C licensed and certified?

Yes, SJB C&C is fully licensed and registered. We hold PEC (Pakistan Engineering Council) registration, ISO 9001:2015 certification for quality management, OHSAS 18001 for occupational health and safety, and LEED certification for green building practices. We are also registered with all relevant local and provincial authorities.
